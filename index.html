FAQ

Common questions from business owners.

Do I need a website if I already use Facebook or Instagram?

Social media is useful, but it doesn't give you full control of the customer experience. A website gives your business a dedicated place to explain what you do, build trust, share reviews, and make it easy for people to get in touch.

How much does a website cost?

Every project is different. Pricing depends on the number of pages, functionality, design requirements and content. We'll provide a clear quote after understanding what you need.

How long does a website take?

Project timelines depend on the size and complexity of the website and how quickly content and feedback are provided.

Can you look after my website after it's launched?

Yes. HF Studios can provide optional ongoing hosting, maintenance and small website updates.

Can you help with the content?

Yes. Depending on the project, we can help organise, improve or create website content.
